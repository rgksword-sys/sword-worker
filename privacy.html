Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

SWORD Worker is an internal automation tool built and operated by SWORD for its own Discord community operations. It is not a public product and is not used by anyone outside the SWORD team.

What it does

Creates and manages Discord giveaway raffles on Alphabot, and records raffle winners in a private Google Sheet used internally by the SWORD team.

Data it accesses

  • Discord server data needed to post raffle announcements and read collab requests.
  • A private Google Sheets spreadsheet owned by the SWORD team.
  • Public raffle data from the Alphabot API.

What it does not do

  • Does not collect data from the general public or raffle participants beyond what Alphabot itself processes.
  • Does not sell or share data, or use it for advertising.
  • Does not access any Google account or Drive file outside the single spreadsheet shared with it.
